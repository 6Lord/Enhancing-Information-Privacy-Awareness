About PromptSmart

Learn to use AI without giving away more than you mean to.

PromptSmart is an educational resource for learners using generative AI for schoolwork. It teaches information privacy through explanations, school-based examples and practical steps—not just a list of rules.

Our purpose

Help learners make informed choices when AI is part of learning.

Generative AI can help explain a difficult idea, brainstorm or improve a draft. Using it may also involve entering prompts, documents or personal details into a service operated by another organisation. The privacy implications depend on the tool, account, settings and applicable school rules.

PromptSmart teaches learners to recognise information, ask how a tool handles it, share only what is necessary and check AI-generated content. The lessons support informed decisions; they do not replace a school’s policy, a service’s terms or advice from a qualified educator.

The essential definitions

Privacy and information privacy

What is privacy?

Privacy is the ability to have boundaries and make informed choices about your personal life, communications and activities without unnecessary intrusion.

It gives people space, dignity and control over who can observe them or make decisions about them.

What is information privacy?

Information privacy is the responsible handling of data that identifies or describes a person. It covers collection, use, sharing, storage, security and deletion.

Examples include a name, ID number, location, photo, marks, health detail, message, device ID or online behaviour.

Why it matters in our context

AI privacy affects real learners

A learner might paste a marked assignment, class list, private message or health-related explanation into an AI assistant to get help. The information could identify the learner or someone else. Depending on the service and its settings, prompts or files may be processed, retained or reviewed under the provider’s rules. A confident AI response may also repeat details or make unfair assumptions. These possibilities make checking first and sharing less important.

Our principles

Useful, ethical and inclusive

Plain language

Technical ideas are explained without unnecessary jargon, with key terms introduced when they matter.

Respect for people

Privacy is connected to dignity, safety, consent and the right to make informed choices.

Practice over panic
