About PromptSmart

Confidence, not complexity.

PromptSmart turns important privacy and AI concepts into clear, useful decisions for learners, families, educators and community members.

Our purpose

Everyone deserves to understand what happens to their information.

Many people use digital services every day without knowing what data is collected, who can access it or how long it remains available. PromptSmart provides a calm place to ask those questions.

We use familiar examples, short explanations and progressive challenges so beginners can start comfortably and more experienced learners can keep thinking critically.

The essential definitions

Privacy and information privacy

What is privacy?

Privacy is the ability to have boundaries and make informed choices about your personal life, communications and activities without unnecessary intrusion.

It gives people space, dignity and control over who can observe them or make decisions about them.

What is information privacy?

Information privacy is the responsible handling of data that identifies or describes a person. It covers collection, use, sharing, storage, security and deletion.

Examples include a name, ID number, location, photo, marks, health detail, message, device ID or online behaviour.

Why it matters in our context

Privacy failures have human consequences

Schools, families and community organisations handle information about real people. A leaked class list, exposed location or copied private message can lead to embarrassment, scams, harassment, discrimination or identity theft.
