How to use a resource critically

  • Check who published it and when it was updated.
  • Look for evidence, definitions and links to primary sources.
  • Compare advice with local laws, your employer’s policies and the exact AI tool’s current terms.
  • Remember that PromptSmart’s learning material is general education, not legal advice or permission to use a particular tool.
  • Ask a manager, IT/privacy contact or qualified adviser when the stakes are high.
